Why Mauritius

A tropical escape with real character.

Mauritius gives international travellers the rare combination of easy resort luxury and a genuinely varied island to explore.

You can wake up beside the Indian Ocean, spend the day discovering dramatic landscapes or sailing a lagoon, then return for a spa treatment, beautiful dinner and sunset by the beach.
