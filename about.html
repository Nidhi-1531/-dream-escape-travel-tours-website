About Dream Escape

Travel planning with a personal touch.

Dream Escape Travel & Tours is an online travel business created to make planning feel simpler, more personal and more inspiring.

Our approach

Your trip should fit you — not the other way around.

We help bring together travel elements such as flights, accommodation, holiday packages, cruises, honeymoons, transfers, excursions, visa assistance and travel insurance guidance. Instead of publishing fixed prices, we prepare enquiries around live availability because travel prices can change.

Whether you already know exactly where you want to go or only know the feeling you want from your holiday, Dream Escape can help you turn the idea into a clearer travel plan.
